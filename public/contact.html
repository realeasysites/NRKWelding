Questions

Before you reach out

How quickly can I get a quote?

Most quotes are provided within 24 to 48 hours after we assess your project details. Estimates are free and come with no obligation.

Do you offer emergency mobile welding?

Yes. Our mobile team handles emergency repairs and structural welding on-site, at your home, farm, job site, or business. Call (207) 899-6084 for the fastest response.

What metals do you work with?

Steel, aluminum, stainless steel, wrought iron, cast iron, and other specialty metals, using MIG, TIG, stick, and flux-core processes.

Are your projects covered by a warranty?

Yes. Our custom handrails, staircases, and structural steel pieces are backed by a lifetime structural warranty.

What is the difference between a floating staircase and a standard steel staircase?

A floating (open-riser) staircase uses a hidden structural support, typically a mono stringer, so each tread appears to hover without visible side supports. A standard steel staircase has exposed stringers on both sides. Both are fully structural and built to Maine building codes, and we fabricate both.

Can you help design a custom project?

Absolutely. Bring an idea, a sketch, or full drawings. We can help with design input, fabrication planning, and shop-ready plans from start to finish.

Do you work with contractors and builders?

Yes. We regularly support general contractors, builders, architects, and interior designers with custom steel, railings, and structural fabrication.

Is there a travel fee for mobile welding?

Our pricing is transparent. Your estimate includes any necessary travel fee and the cost of the repair before we start work.
